This research proposes the Artificial Potential Field-Enhanced Improved Multiobjective Snake Optimization (APF-IMOSO) algorithm to overcome the premature convergence and lack of diversity observed in traditional meta-heuristic path planning. The method integrates four key enhancements to the core algorithm. For effective dynamic path planning, APF-IMOSO introduces an equal nodes partitioning approach and incorporates the Artificial Potential Field (APF) method as a dedicated fitness function, enabling the simultaneous optimization of path length, safety, energy consumption, and time efficiency. Extensive comparative simulations, yielding up to a 50.71% improvement in energy efficiency over the original Snake Optimization algorithm, combined with real-robot verification, confirm the method’s efficacy. Ultimately, the results reveal that APF-IMOSO can generate multiple viable collision-free paths in complex environments under various constraints, showcasing its potential in robot navigation.
