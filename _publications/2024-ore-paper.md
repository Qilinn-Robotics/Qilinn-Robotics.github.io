In metal ore mining, detecting and locating large ore targets for secondary blasting is challenging due to adverse conditions such as high dust levels, dense ore distributions, and low edge contrasts. This paper introduces a multi-ore target detection and positioning method that combines monocular vision with LiDAR technology. Integrating LiDAR distance data with industrial camera pixel information and applying monocular vision principles, we achieve precise detection and positioning of multiple ore targets. The RGB images captured by the industrial camera are processed using an improved Mask R-CNN (Mask Regional Convolutional Neural Network) algorithm. The detection performance of the proposed method was validated using a self-constructed dataset. The experimental results demonstrate that the MioU (mean Intersection over Union) and mAP (mean Average Precision) for multi-ore targets reached 84.9% and 91.7%, respectively, surpassing the original algorithm’s performance of 76.0% and 81.7%. The relative error in the lateral distance positioning of multi-ore targets is 3.52%. This method can more effectively accomplish real-time ore target detection and positioning in complex field environment.
